Whether you need eco friendly corporate gifts for a single event or an ongoing employee gifting programme, the same process applies: share your requirement, and our team develops a shortlist of sustainable, customizable corporate gifts around your budget and quantity.

FAQ

Corporate gifting, answered

Do you accept bulk corporate gifting orders?

Yes. Vikri is built around bulk and custom corporate orders rather than single-piece purchases. Share your quantity and timeline through the enquiry form and our team will confirm feasibility.

Can products be customized with our company logo?

Logo and brand customization is available on a number of products, depending on the item, material and order quantity. Tell us your requirement and we'll confirm what's possible.

Can products be customized according to our budget?

We work with a range of products and combinations, so options can be shaped around a stated budget. Share your approximate budget in the enquiry form for the most relevant suggestions.

Can packaging be customized?

Packaging can be developed around the gifting requirement where available. Let us know your occasion and branding needs and we'll advise on options.

Do you provide samples?

Where applicable, samples or design approval can be arranged before bulk production begins. This is confirmed case by case depending on the order.

Do you deliver across India?

Vikri works with corporate and bulk gifting customers across India. Share your delivery location and timeline and we'll confirm coordination for your order.

How early should we place a bulk order?

Lead time depends on the product, customization and quantity involved. Reach out as early as possible for large or heavily customized orders so there's enough time for samples, production and delivery.

Can we combine different products in a gift set?

Yes, product combinations are part of how Vikri builds hampers and gift sets. Share the occasion, budget and recipient profile and we'll help put together a combination.
